# Welcome to Bookfish

The guide that comes with the app: a short readaloud that explains Bookfish
and lets the reader try each feature on its page. It is always in the
library (author "Finn Bookfish") and opens on the first start.

- `welcome-to-bookfish.md`: the text, with marks for the footnote, the
  picture, the table, the link and the parts that are not narrated.
- `welcome-to-bookfish.epub`: the readaloud built from it. The narration is
  an AI voice (Google Gemini text to speech, voice Zephyr), aligned with the
  text sentence by sentence by the maintainer's own aligner, which works like
  [Storyteller](https://storyteller-platform.dev/) with speech recognition.
  The pictures were made with Google Gemini (goldfish) and ImageMagick
  (folder tree).

The text was written by an AI from the maintainer's outline and comments,
and approved by him.
Text, pictures and audio are under the same license as the app,
GPL-3.0-or-later.
